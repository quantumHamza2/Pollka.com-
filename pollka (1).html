How the Allocation Algorithm Works
1
Preference Order Priority: All students' 1st-choice subjects are processed first, then 2nd-choice, and so on.
2
CGPA Tiebreaker: When multiple students compete for the same subject in the same round, the student with the higher CGPA is allocated first.
3
Seat Availability: A subject is only allocated if it still has remaining seats at the time of processing.
4
Re-runnable: Running allocation again clears previous results and recalculates from scratch.
